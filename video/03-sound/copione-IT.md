# SKYVALE WORLD  ·  Episode 3: bm Sound — narrator's script

Each line starts at the time shown (final video).

- **0:12** bm Sound makes the sounds, the sound effects and the music of a cartridge: the AUDIO section of the .bm, the same bank the games play with sfx and music.
- **0:18** Ctrl+O opens the cartridge from episode 2.
- **0:22** Four pages: F1 the sounds, the instruments; F2 the sound effects; F3 patterns, a step sequencer with eight tracks; F4 the songs.
- **0:32** A sound is an instrument: a wave, an envelope and a pitch, then a filter, then room and echo. The first one will be the jump. Enter names it.
- **0:36** Down goes into the values and left or right between the columns. For a jump the pitch starts low and slides up: BEND FROM minus twelve, over a tenth of a second.
- **0:37** I plays the sound as it is: listen to the slide.
- **0:40** PgDn is the next sound: the coin. Another wave, a pluck, and a name.
- **0:47** And the third, the stomp on an enemy: noise with a quick drop in pitch.
- **0:53** F2 is for sound effects: up to 32 steps, each a note of a sound. The keyboard is a piano: the lower letters are one octave, the upper row the next.
- **0:56** The jump: four notes going up, played with the jump sound. Each key places a step and moves on.
- **0:59** Up selects the header of the effect: Enter names it, and with minus or underscore the speed changes: a step lasts 60 milliseconds, here faster.
- **1:05** PgDn is the next effect: the coin, two high notes. The sound for new steps is the one chosen on F1.
- **1:12** And the stomp: three notes falling.
- **1:19** For the music, F7 opens riff: a pattern written in one line, in the style of live-coding music. Drums, a bass, a melody with the chip instrument.
- **1:27** Enter plays it live: the words light up as the notes sound.
- **1:41** Ctrl+Enter puts four bars of it into the bank as a song, with its patterns.
- **1:43** The patterns are the steps on eight tracks, one voice each: riff wrote them, and they can be edited by hand. The song orders them.
- **1:50** Space plays the song from the bank, the way a game plays it with music.
- **2:07** Ctrl+S saves the bank into the .bm. In a game: sfx for the effects, music for the song. Next time: bm Code, to make Kip move.
