# SKYVALE WORLD  ·  Episode 1: bm Pixel — narrator's script

Italian version: [copione-IT.md](copione-IT.md).

Each line starts at the time shown (final video).

- **0:12** bm Pixel is the pixel-art editor inside the console. Open it from the Dev tab; it works on the sprite sheet of a .bm.
- **0:17** The menu: open a cartridge, start a new sheet, save, or try the game with F5.
- **0:21** Three pages: F1 to draw, F2 for the whole sheet, F3 for the palette.
- **0:29** Z changes the size of the sprite: 8, 16, 32, 64 up to 128 pixels. We will draw at 16 by 16.
- **0:36** Tab opens the list of commands of the page. Hold F12 to see the keys at any time.
- **0:42** Our hero is Kip, a fox. Like the heroes of the side-scrolling platformers, he is seen from the side, running to the right.
- **0:42** Colour 17 is the dark outline. 0 is the tenth colour, and the dot key walks to the next ones.
- **0:43** Arrows move the pointer, Space paints a pixel. First the outline, pixel by pixel.
- **0:51** G is the fill: choose the orange, move inside the outline, Space. One press fills the whole body, the tip of the tail needs another.
- **0:54** Back to the pencil for the details: the white muzzle, the pink ears and the blue scarf.
- **0:59** A mistake? Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes. Even a whole fill.
- **1:05** A run needs more than one picture. The sprites that follow in the sheet are the frames of an animation: copy Kip with Ctrl+C, PgDn for the next sprite, Ctrl+V to paste.
- **1:07** Then only what changes: the eraser takes pixels away, the pencil adds the new ones. Legs, tail, a pixel of bounce.
- **1:27** The same again for each pose of the run: contact, down, passing, flight. Time-lapse: five more frames and a jump.
- **2:14** Back to the first frame with PgUp. The panel on the right plays the frames in a loop: plus sets how many (six for the run), the speed keys change the rate.
- **2:26** K is the onion skin: the frame before shows through, to check each step against the last.
- **2:35** Next object: a coin. A coin is symmetric, so this time the mirror is useful: Y turns it on.
- **2:38** O draws a filled oval: Space fixes one corner, the arrows go to the opposite corner, Space again.
- **2:42** The same oval one pixel smaller, in gold, leaves the dark rim.
- **2:45** Now Y again: the shine is on one side only. L draws a straight line between two points.
- **2:49** U is a filled rectangle: the slot in the middle of the coin.
- **2:52** F2 shows the whole sheet: the six frames of the run, the jump and the coin side by side. Arrows choose a sprite, Enter draws it.
- **3:01** F3 is the palette: up to 256 colours. The same palette is read by bm Studio and the games.
- **3:07** Save as with Ctrl+Shift+S. The name asked is already filled in: Backspace clears it, then we type skyvale.
- **3:11** Enter saves: the sheet becomes a cartridge, skyvale.bm, with a code that shows it.
- **3:14** F5 runs the cartridge: here, its sprite sheet. Next time: bm Studio, tiles and the first level.

Skyvale World is a 2D platformer built only with the tools inside the bm console. In this episode we draw its hero, Kip, with bm Pixel: pencil, fill, a six-frame run cycle, animation preview, mirror and shapes, palette and saving a .bm.
