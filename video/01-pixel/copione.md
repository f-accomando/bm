# SKYVALE WORLD  ·  Episode 1: bm Pixel — narrator's script

Each line starts at the time shown (final video).

- **0:12** bm Pixel is the pixel-art editor inside the console. Open it from the Dev tab; it works on the sprite sheet of a .bm.
- **0:17** The menu: open a cartridge, start a new sheet, save, or try the game with F5.
- **0:21** Three pages: F1 to draw, F2 for the whole sheet, F3 for the palette.
- **0:29** Z changes the size of the sprite: 8, 16, 32, 64 up to 128 pixels. We will draw at 16 by 16.
- **0:36** Tab opens the list of commands of the page. Hold F12 to see the keys at any time.
- **0:42** Kip is symmetric, so we turn on the mirror with Y: everything on the left appears on the right too.
- **0:44** Colour 17 is the dark outline. 0 is the tenth colour, and the dot key walks to the next ones.
- **0:45** Arrows move the pointer, Space paints. Only the left half: the mirror does the rest.
- **0:52** G is the fill: choose the orange, move inside the outline, Space. One press fills the whole body.
- **0:54** Back to the pencil for the details: the white muzzle, the pink ears and the blue scarf.
- **0:58** A mistake? Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes. Even a whole fill.
- **1:05** The sprites that follow in the sheet are the frames of an animation. Copy Kip with Ctrl+C, go to the next sprite with PgDn, paste with Ctrl+V.
- **1:09** Only the legs change: the eraser on the old feet, the pencil for the new ones. The mirror is still on.
- **1:22** The panel on the right plays the frames: plus and minus change how many, the arrows of the speed keys make it faster or slower, P pauses.
- **1:31** Next sprite, next object: a coin. PgDn moves on, and Y turns the mirror off, because a coin highlight is not symmetric.
- **1:33** O draws a filled oval: Space fixes one corner, the arrows go to the opposite corner, Space again.
- **1:37** The same oval one pixel smaller, in yellow, leaves the dark rim.
- **1:41** L draws a straight line between two points: a white shine.
- **1:43** U is a filled rectangle: the slot in the middle of the coin.
- **1:46** Four tools, one coin: and the mirror is a single key away when you need it again.
- **1:49** F2 shows the whole sheet: our four frames are side by side. Arrows choose a sprite, Enter draws it.
- **1:55** F3 is the palette: up to 256 colours. The same palette is read by bm Studio and the games.
- **2:01** Save as with Ctrl+Shift+S: the sheet becomes a cartridge, skyvale.bm, with a code that shows it.
- **2:08** F5 runs the cartridge: here, its sprite sheet. Next time: bm Studio, tiles and the first level.

Skyvale World is a 2D platformer built only with the tools inside the bm console. In this episode we draw its hero, Kip, with bm Pixel: pencil, fill, mirror, animation frames, palette and saving a .bm.
