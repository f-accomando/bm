# Melodies for the music assistant's network (scripts/trainmusic.py).
#
# Only tunes in the public domain (traditional, or by composers dead for
# more than 70 years), written down here from memory for bm (a melody,
# simplified, not anyone's edition), and tunes written for bm itself
# ("bm" in the title). They teach the network how a melody moves over the
# chords; it never copies them.
#
# == id               the start of a tune
# title:              what it is
# mode:               major or minor
# meter:              sixteenths in a bar (16 = 4/4, 12 = 3/4 or 6/8, 8 = 2/4)
# chords:             one per bar, between |; two in a bar ("I V") take a half each;
#                     roman numerals on the scale: I ii iii IV V vi vii (major),
#                     i ii III iv v V VI VII (minor)
# notes:              degrees of the scale 1..7, '#' or 'b' before for a sharp or a
#                     flat, ' or , after for an octave up or down, :n the length in
#                     sixteenths (4 if missing), r a rest; bars between |. A tune
#                     can have several notes: lines.
