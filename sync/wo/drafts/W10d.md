## Change (no open design choices)
1. `src/bm/runtime.c`: a member `r3d_pfx_t *pfx;` in `rt` (next to `fade`, `:143`); `static int l_particles(lua_State *L)`: `particles(max)` frees any pool, creates `r3d_pfx_new((int)luaL_optinteger(L, 1, 1024))`, pushes the granted capacity (`rt.pfx ? rt.pfx->cap : 0`); `l_particles_add`: `particles_add(x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, life, [rgb])` (`rgb` default `0xFFFFFF`; `life` in updates, i.e. frames; no pool: returns nil), pushes the slot; `l_particles_step`: `particles_step([gravity])` (default 0.0) calls `r3d_pfx_step(&rt.r3d, rt.pfx, gravity)`; `l_particles_draw`: `particles_draw([radius])` (default 0.1) returns `r3d_pfx_draw(&rt.r3d, rt.pfx, radius)` (the pixels); `l_particles_count`: live count. All no-ops returning 0 without a pool. Register the five in the table at `:4757` next to `point3d`. Free the pool where `rt.fade` is freed (`:7439` area): `r3d_pfx_free(rt.pfx); rt.pfx = NULL;`, and also when a new cartridge starts if `rt` is reset there (search the reset with `cindex`; if it zeroes `rt`, the free must come first: say what you found).
2. `src/script/bmlib.lua`, after `Parts:clear` (`:799` area), a section `----- particles3d`: `function lib.particles3d(maxn)` calls `particles(maxn or 1024)` and returns `setmetatable({}, P3)`; methods: `P3:add(x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, life, color)` -> `particles_add(...)`; `P3:burst(x, y, z, n, o)` with `o.speed` (2), `o.life` (30 frames, each 0.7..1.3 of it), `o.color` (0xFFFFFF), `o.up` (0): n particles with a random direction on the sphere (`a = random() * lib.TAU; u = random() * 2 - 1; s = sqrt(1 - u*u)`, velocity `speed * (0.3 + 0.7 * random()) * (cos(a) * s, u + up, sin(a) * s)`); `P3:update(gravity)` -> `particles_step(gravity or 0.02)`; `P3:draw(radius)` -> `particles_draw(radius or 0.1)`; `P3:count()` -> `particles_count()`. The random numbers come from the file's `random()`, as `Parts:burst` does.
