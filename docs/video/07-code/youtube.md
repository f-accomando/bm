## Descrizione

```
Tropic Trail is a 3D platformer built only with the tools inside the bm console. In this episode we write the game itself in bm Code: Pico runs with the stick, falls and jumps on the map's ground, spins with the B button, collects apples, breaks crates, and follows the camera. The console's own AI assistant (F6) gives us the third-person camera.

What you will see:
- loading the world and the models (map3d_build, map3d_objects, model)
- movement, gravity, walls and holes (stick, map3d_height, map3d_blocked)
- jump and spin with keymap, and the animation clips (animate)
- apples by distance, crates broken by a spin or a landing, the score HUD
- lights, shadow and fog every frame, and the camera from the assistant
```
