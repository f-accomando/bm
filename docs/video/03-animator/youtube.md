## Descrizione

Pico the gecko learns to move. In this episode of Tropic Trail, a 3D platformer built only with the tools inside the bm console,
we open Pico in bm Animator: we build a nine-bone skeleton, skin the model, and record five clips with keyframes: idle and run (loops),
jump, spin and hit. Then we play them back, blend one into another and save.

You will see: the rig page (new bone, move the ends, mirror left to right), the skin (each face or each corner follows its nearest bone),
the timeline at 12 frames per second, loop on and off, linear and smooth movement, onion skin, and saving an editable copy.

Tropic Trail is a series of 8 videos. Each one teaches one editor of the bm console and takes a step towards the same finished game.
Next video: the map editor, where we lay out the trail Pico runs along.

bm: https://github.com/f-accomando/bm
