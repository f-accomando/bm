A SNES ROM has a small table of facts at the end of its first 32 KB bank. In a LoROM this table is at $FFC0 to $FFFF of bank 0.

## The header
The header starts at $FFC0. It has the game title (21 letters), the map mode (LoROM), the chip type, the ROM size, the RAM size, the region and a checksum. You do not write it by hand. The builder of the SDK writes it when you press Ctrl+B. The title comes from the name of your project.

## The vectors
The last bytes, from $FFE0 to $FFFF, are the vectors. A vector is the address of a routine. When something special happens, the CPU reads the right vector and jumps there. In native mode the main ones are: COP at $FFE4, BRK at $FFE6, ABORT at $FFE8, NMI at $FFEA, IRQ at $FFEE. The reset vector is at $FFFC and it uses the emulation mode table: the CPU always starts there after power on.

The builder fills the vectors for you. It looks for labels with the names reset, nmi, irq, brk and cop in your source. A vector with no label points to a small routine that only returns (RTI).

The program below reads the NMI vector from the ROM and compares it with the address of the label nmi. If they are equal, it writes 1 to $0012.

```asm expect=w12=01
.lorom
.org $8000
reset:
    sei
    clc
    xce
    rep #$38
    ldx #$1FFF
    txs
    sep #$20
    lda $00FFEA         ; low byte of the NMI vector
    sta $10
    lda $00FFEB         ; high byte
    sta $11
    lda $10
    cmp #<nmi           ; low byte of the address of the label nmi
    bne bad
    lda $11
    cmp #>nmi           ; high byte of the same address
    bne bad
    lda #$01            ; same: write 1
    sta $12
    bra forever
bad:
    lda #$02            ; different: write 2
    sta $12
forever:
    bra forever
nmi:
    rti                 ; the routine the NMI vector points to
```
The signs < and > take the low and the high byte of a 16 bit value. The sign ^ takes the bank.

try: Rename the label nmi to vblank. The builder then cannot find it and uses its own RTI, so the program writes 2.

## What you learned
The header and the vectors are at the end of bank 0. The vectors point to your routines, and the builder finds them by name.
